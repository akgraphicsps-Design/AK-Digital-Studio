Delivery Framework

Discovery & Architecture

Deliverable: Mental model diagrams & IA maps
PHASE 02

Low-Fidelity Validation

Deliverable: Clickable structural prototype

PHASE 04

Production Handoff & QA

Deliverable: Production-ready CSS/Storybook support
